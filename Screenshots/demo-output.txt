3. Filters that read hidden scalars as part of the text
=======================================================
[card, plain]
  raw text:      "4111 1111 1111 1111"
  standard:      "4111 1111 1111 1111"
  matching view: "4111 1111 1111 1111"
[card, ZWSP between groups]
  raw text:      no match
  standard:      "4111111111111111"
  matching view: "4111111111111111"
[card, word joiner in a group]
  raw text:      no match
  standard:      "4111 1111 1111 1111"
  matching view: "4111 1111 1111 1111"
[card, tag inside the number]
  raw text:      "4111⟦U+E0078⟧ 1111 1111 1111"
  standard:      "4111 1111 1111 1111"
  matching view: "4111 1111 1111 1111"
[email, ZWNJ in the local part]
  raw text:      ".doe@example.com"
  standard:      "jane.doe@example.com"
  matching view: "jane.doe@example.com"
[email, soft hyphen in a word]
  raw text:      "e@example.com"
  standard:      "e@example.com"
  matching view: "jane.doe@example.com"

A soft hyphen between two letters is legitimate, so the standard policy keeps it and the
detector still matches only part of the address. Detectors need the .matching view;
display and the model keep the standard one.
